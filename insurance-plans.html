Core Coverages

The essentials for protecting your car.

Comprehensive Insurance

Wide cover for the unexpected

This is our broadest cover option, designed to protect your vehicle from accidents, theft, and other unexpected damage. It also helps with legal costs when you are held responsible for an incident on the road.

Key Benefits

  • Damage Cover: Helps with repair or replacement costs after accidents, theft, or other covered events.
  • Roadside Support: Help is available when you need a tow, emergency assistance, or basic roadside help.
  • Legal Protection: Can help with legal costs if a claim or accident leads to a dispute.

Exclusions

  • Mechanical Issues: Routine wear and tear or faults unrelated to an accident are not covered.
  • Personal Belongings: Items left in the car, such as electronics or valuables, are not included.
  • Impaired Driving: Cover does not apply when the driver is under the influence or otherwise not fit to drive.

Third-Party Liability (TPL)

Cover for damage caused to others

This plan helps when your vehicle causes damage to someone else’s property or injures another person. It is designed to protect you from the financial side of those claims, but it does not cover damage to your own vehicle.

Key Benefits

  • Injury Claims: Helps with medical costs and related expenses for third parties injured by your vehicle.
  • Funeral Support: Offers financial support in serious cases involving a third party’s death.
  • Property Damage: Helps cover repairs or losses caused to other people’s property.

Exclusions

  • Your Own Vehicle: Repairs or replacement costs for your own car are not included.
  • Your Own Injury: Medical or funeral costs for the insured driver are excluded.
  • Unapproved Use: Cover does not apply for racing, illegal use, or driving an unregistered vehicle.

Personal Accident Coverage

Support for you and your passengers

If you or a passenger is injured in a road accident, this cover helps with medical expenses and the financial pressure that can come with serious injury. It is designed to give you support when you need it most.

Key Benefits

  • Accidental Death Benefit: A lump-sum payment may be made to your beneficiaries if a road accident leads to death.
  • Disability Support: Helps with income and expenses if a serious injury leaves you unable to work.
  • Medical Costs: Covers hospital and treatment expenses arising from an accident.

Exclusions

  • Illegal Activity: Cover does not apply to criminal or unlawful acts.
  • Invalid License: Claims are excluded if the driver was not properly licensed.
  • Impaired Condition: Driving under the influence or while physically or mentally unfit is not covered.

Supplemental Benefits

Extra protection for the things standard cover does not fully handle.

Add-on Coverage

Extra cover for the gaps in standard plans

Add-on coverage gives you more protection beyond your standard policy. It is useful when you want to cover items or situations that are not fully included in your main insurance.

Key Benefits

  • Personal Effects Coverage: Helps replace or repair belongings kept inside your vehicle.
  • Loss of Use Coverage: Supports temporary transport or daily allowance while your car is being repaired.
  • Zero Depreciation: Covers replacement costs without reducing for vehicle depreciation.

Exclusions

  • Unauthorized Drivers: Cover does not apply if someone drives without permission or without a valid license.
  • Mechanical Breakdown: Engine or mechanical faults are not covered unless specified elsewhere.
  • Pre-existing Damage: Damage that was already present before the add-on was purchased is excluded.
