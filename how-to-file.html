How to File a New Claim

  1. Make sure everyone is safe. Call emergency services if anyone is hurt.
  2. Gather your documents. Use the checklist below so you have everything ready.
  3. Verify your policy. Enter your policy number and details.
  4. Fill in your contact and personal information.
  5. Describe what happened. Include the date, time, and location of the incident.
  6. Upload photos and documents as digital evidence.
  7. Review and submit. You will receive a claim reference number.
